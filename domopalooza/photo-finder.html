Photo Finder

Domopalooza 2026 - Find and add missing attendee photos

Workflow

  1. Click LinkedIn to find their profile photo (best source), or Google Images as fallback
  2. Save the photo as {slug}.jpg and {slug}-lg.jpg into the photos/ folder. The slug is shown in purple on each card.
  3. Click Done on each person when you've saved their photo (tracked in localStorage)
  4. When finished, run python3 update-photos.py to wire new photos into the main lookup tool

Tip: For Tier A, do them one at a time. For Tier B, use "Open All LinkedIn" to batch-open tabs.
